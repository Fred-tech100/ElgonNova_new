OUR STORY

Pioneering Digital Excellence in East Africa

ElgonNova Technologies was founded with a vision to use technology to transform businesses, empower communities, and contribute to Africa’s digital growth. Based in Mbale, Uganda, we started with a passion for innovation and a commitment to delivering reliable, affordable, and modern technology solutions. From website and software development to IoT, graphics design, IT consultancy, computer repair, data analysis, and professional printing services, we strive to help businesses and individuals succeed in the digital age. As we grow, we aim to expand into areas such as machine learning, cybersecurity, networking, and computer training to further support Africa’s technological future. We believe Africa’s challenges can be solved through African innovation, and we are committed to building technology solutions that create lasting impact across the continent.

OUR MISSION

To deliver innovative, reliable, and affordable technology solutions that empower businesses, organizations, and communities across Africa. We are committed to providing high-quality services in software and website development, IoT, graphics design, IT consultancy, data analysis, computer repair and maintenance, and professional printing while embracing emerging technologies such as machine learning, cybersecurity, networking, and digital training to drive Africa’s technological growth and transformation.

OUR VISION

To become Africa’s leading technology innovation company, empowering businesses, institutions, and communities through smart digital solutions that drive sustainable development, economic transformation, and technological independence across the continent. At ElgonNova Technologies, we envision an Africa where technology is not only consumed but created locally to solve African challenges, inspire innovation, and unlock opportunities for future generations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with ElgonNova Technologies.

How long does it take to build a website?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. A simple landing page can be completed in 3–5 days. A full business website typically takes 2–4 weeks. Complex e-commerce or web applications may take 4–12 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline estimate during our initial consultation.

Do you offer ongoing support and maintenance?

Yes! We offer comprehensive maintenance packages that include regular updates, security monitoring, performance optimization, content updates, and 24/7 technical support. Our maintenance plans are affordable and customizable to your specific needs.

What technologies do you use for software development?

We use a wide range of modern technologies including React, Vue.js, Node.js, Python, Django, PHP/Laravel, Flutter for mobile apps, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and cloud platforms like AWS and Google Cloud. We select the best technology stack based on your specific project requirements.

How do you handle data privacy and security?

Data security is our top priority. We implement industry-standard security practices including SSL encryption, secure coding practices, regular security audits, data backup systems, and compliance with relevant data protection regulations. We sign NDAs when required to protect client confidentiality.

What is your pricing model?

We offer flexible pricing models including fixed-price projects, hourly rates, and retainer agreements. All pricing is transparent with no hidden fees. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your requirements. Payment plans are available for larger projects. Contact us for a free consultation and quote.

Can you work with clients outside Mbale or Uganda?

Absolutely! While we are based in Mbale, Uganda, we serve clients across East Africa and internationally. We have successfully completed remote projects for clients in Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, and beyond. We use modern collaboration tools to ensure seamless communication regardless of location.
